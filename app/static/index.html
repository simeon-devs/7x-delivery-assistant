How to look at it

Open both, side by side. Escalate in one and the case appears in the other without a refresh.

Of 840 real shipments, only 201 can have an address changed safely. The rest are closed, blocked, or their two records contradict each other. That scope came from the data, not from a preference.

What is not real

Shipment records are synthetic, supplied with the assignment.

The WhatsApp channel is simulated, not connected to the WhatsApp Business API, and no SMS is ever really sent. The agent, the rules and the database writes are real.
